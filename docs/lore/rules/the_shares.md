## Function

The economy rule-of-record (M2 economy pass, director decisions):
money, the binding scarcity, the takings on goods, the season's stop,
and the black market's shape. Design input for pricing wherever specs
touch trade — salvage, shops, tolls, rewards — with the numbers
themselves living in balance data, never here.

## Canon summary

Market cash is the bead: worked and blessed lava-glass strung at the
wrist, paid by untying — wealth is worn where it can be counted, and a
bare wrist is poverty everyone can read. Above the bead sit three
credit layers that do not convert cleanly — parish heat-hours, crew
shares, and sealed roll-notes — and moneychanging between them is a
trade. The binding scarcity is heat: the vents' output is finite and
fully apportioned, so the island cannot grow without someone
shrinking. Goods pay three takings — the crown's toll at Lastdoor's
gate, the parish tithe at assessment, and the sea's share tipped back
at the truce-lines — and the bridge collects a fourth no crown has
ever collected an answer for. Roughly once a year, for weeks, high-sky
season seals the gates: the season is real, its edges are warden
declarations, and the pre-seal rush is the market's loudest fortnight.

## Visible traits

Wrist-strings counted at a glance; changers' tables chalked in three
columns. Toll-lights and lamp-tolls paid in kind on the roads.
Quarantine rows of salvage awaiting the assessors. The tip at the
truce-line, unwitnessed by custom. Pre-seal markets stripped to the
boards, and notice-boards thick with seal-rumor while the wardens say
nothing yet.

## Systems impact

Pricing logic (the design input): anything from the open is priced by
the exposure spent fetching it; anything vent-made is priced in the
heat-hours it took; anything moved accumulates tolls as it travels —
Lastdoor's salvage pays twice between the fields and a Highroof shelf,
the standing double-toll grievance. Settlement roof-tax and ward tolls
stay local law ([[the_taking]], [[highroof]]), outside the takings on
trade. Wealth is worn, so standing is
readable at arm's length and theft is intimate. When the seal comes
down, apportionment is the whole market: the parish cords set every
table, warehousers and the galleries' premium do the rest, the crown's
toll revenue gaps, and idle crews drift toward Quiet Line work — the
sealed weeks are the parishes' weeks, and everyone knows it. The black
market's supply is unmanifested salvage (and scrimshaw, the old
standby); its venue is the galleries of the Scar; its wash is a parish
key — goods entered for assessment vanish from the quarantine row
overnight and re-enter the market blessed. The Bell Rolls are public
record, parish-kept: anyone may read, only the parish writes.

## Relationships

Prices the world [[the_taking]] made: doctrine's tables set exposure
costs ([[warden_doctrine]]), the [[vent_parishes]] hold tithe,
apportionment, and stall-patronage, the [[licensed_crews]] run shares
and salvage, [[the_crown]] tolls the gate at [[lastdoor]], the
[[chitin_bridge]] takes the fourth taking, and [[tidegate]] tips the
sea's share beneath the [[leviathan_ribs]]. Heat-commons
([[embershare]]) against light-deed ([[undersun_galleries]]) is the
scarcity's legal face — and a tier held empty against heat-starved
housing pressure ([[cold_tier_hush]]) is, in this light, economically
obscene: the mystery has a price tag. The galleries of [[the_scar]]
are the market's shadow; the [[bellworks]] keeps the Rolls behind
glass; the bead is [[the_workings]]' glass wearing its other hat.

## Quest hooks

- A changer is converting crew shares into sealed roll-notes at rates
  no honest ledger survives — funding something that wants clean
  paper.
- A warehouse is cornering fish-oil ahead of a seal declaration that
  has not happened — someone is reading the tallies before the wardens
  speak.
- A boat lands heavier than its share allows. By the next ebb the port
  knows; nobody has said a word to the crew, and the crew is starting
  to understand that the silence is the response.
- The quarantine row emptied on a night the parish key provably never
  left its cabinet — either there is a second key, or the wash has
  learned to run itself.
- The wash took what wasn't offered: a crew's find vanished from
  assessment before they had even decided to hide it — the first crew
  in memory with standing to complain about the black market's
  service.

## Environmental storytelling

Changers' boards with one column left blank — no posted rate today
between shares and notes; the changer decides by face. The drowned
shrine's outward-facing vertebra, where the season's share is vowed.
Pre-seal awning-stubs hung with overflow goods, stalls spilling into
the last covered stretches. A toll-book margin where some clerk
tallied a jar of seed's takings gate to shelf — four hands paid before
a cook ever touches it.
