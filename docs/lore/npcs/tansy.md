## Function

The town's shop anchor and its gentlest informer: a stall of
terrace-fungus simples, first doses, and the occasional daylight
rarity. Strangers get the common courtesy and nothing more until they
are not strangers — the fallback greeting made flesh. She knows who is
sick, who is short, and who is buying odd things.

## Canon summary

Tansy is Lastlight, named from the word-hoard's field plants — a
healer carrying a healing plant of the lost surface, and everyone
hears it. Her stall in [[lastdoor]]'s market row sells fungus simples
grown on [[embershare]]'s terraces and carried down the roads, plus
the rare salvaged herb or seed priced like the treasure it is. By her
own rule she refuses no one a first dose; by Lastlight habit she
remembers exactly who never came back to pay.

## Visible traits

Weathered wraps in defiant fungal brights; strung simples drying
overhead in culture-loud colors; the sky-scarf pinned back for work.
A window-box of true old-world grain stands at her stall's end,
tended like the shrine it is. Found at the stall every market bell;
found at ninth-ebb funerals singing, like every Lastlight voice.

## Systems impact

Sells simples and draughts (the town's healing supply between parish
guest-portions); buys salvaged herbs, seed, and daylight jars from
crews; her first-dose rule makes her stall the place bad news arrives
first. Prices climb politely for the roofbound and drop quietly for
crews just in from the covers.

## Relationships

[[lastlight]] by descent and song; supplied from [[embershare]]'s
terraces; trades with the [[licensed_crews]] for salvaged rarities;
correct, unhurried distance from the parish tithe-assessors
([[vent_parishes]]) — hospitality is their creed, first doses are
hers, and neither says the other's is the copy.

## Quest hooks

- A crew brought her a sealed daylight jar. The seal-text is High
  Register, she will not sell what she cannot name, and the people
  offering to read it all want something — the Column-Readers hold
  the only sanctioned reading right, which is exactly what makes
  their offer suspect ([[reciters]]).
- Someone buys out one simple, every market-day, at asking price,
  without haggling. To a Lastlight stall-keeper this is not custom —
  it is a message she cannot read yet.
- A child's fever answers nothing on her shelves. What would answer
  it grows in a fields herb-plot she knows from her grandmother's
  telling — off-license, off-budget, and she is asking quietly who
  walks the covers well.

## Environmental storytelling

The first-dose ladle worn thin at the lip. Chalk tallies of unpaid
first doses, wiped clean each ninth ebb without comment. The
window-box grain, and the particular way she stands between it and
strangers.
