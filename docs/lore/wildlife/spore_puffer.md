## Function

A ranged hazard of the galleries (spec 024): the spore-puffer holds its
ground and coughs a stinging cloud rather than charging, teaching the
player that not every threat closes to arm's reach. Its spent gland is a
salter's and simpler's reagent.

## Canon summary

Spore-puffers are wild fungus-growths of the roofed dark — kin in kind to
the cultivated terrace fungus the island eats and doctors with, but feral,
unlit, and defensive. Rooted-slow and near-blind, a puffer swells and
releases a dust-cloud when a footfall crosses its patch; the dust stings
the eyes and lungs at a short distance. Salters and simplers gather the
spent gland — the spore-sac — and grind it into draughts, where its dust
is said to thin a worker's regard the way a moth-fall thins a lamp.
