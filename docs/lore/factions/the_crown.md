## Function

Supporting power: the weak center. The crown is not a guild but a
legal address — the arbiter the guilds argue in front of, the rolls
their agreements live in, and the one office with standing to touch
every other. Its weakness is the island's constitution.

## Canon summary

The Crown is the office the [[crown_compact]] wrote carefully small:
arbiter of disputes, keeper of the assent-rolls, owner of almost
nothing — and both tellings of *why* are still recited, because the
compact stays silent. It keeps two strange instruments: the
report-purse for sounds within the [[sealed_reaches]], paid without
interruption for generations, and the struck clause in the compact's
own text, legible, initialed, and never once invoked. The current
crown is the first Lastlight-descended arbiter in living memory — a
quiet scandal the compact never imagined, borne with conspicuous
correctness by all parties.

## Visible traits

The small seal, worn from use on trivial things. Clerk corps in
roll-grey doing the office's real daily work. The arbiter's
sky-scarf, worn indoors, folded — a Lastlight signature in the most
Underfirst room on the island, and everyone notices and no one
remarks.

## Systems impact

Arbitration and precedent: when tables, tithes, and tolls collide,
the crown's hearing is where the island's rival law-systems meet.
The rolls make standing — which is why the player, unrolled and
unprecedented, is a filing problem the clerks have opened a new
drawer for.

## Relationships

Seated at [[highroof]]; recognizes but does not command the
[[wardens]]; funds the listeners at the [[sealed_reaches]]; hears the
[[licensed_crews]]' toll grievances against the bridge-keepers of
[[chitin_bridge]] — whose allegiance the compact left answerable, and
every crown since has declined to answer. Toward
[[the_untaken_arrival]]: the crown wants the player *filed* — met,
recorded, and precedented — before any guild owns them.

## Quest hooks

- The filing: the clerks summon the player to be entered — and the
  form's first question ("under whose roof were you born?") has no
  lawful answer, which three factions would each like resolved their
  way.
- The missing listener (see [[highroof]]): the report and its
  reporter vanished between the boundary gallery and the purse-clerk's
  desk — inside the crown's own corridor.
- The struck clause: a guild hearing cites it for the first time in
  the compact's history, to see what the arbiter does.

## Environmental storytelling

The assent-rolls' shelf, one volume per generation, thinnest in the
oldest years. The purse-ledger's uninterrupted line of payments,
rendered in a clerk-hand that changes every few decades and never
misses a season. The arbiter's folded sky-scarf on its own hook,
beside the seal.
