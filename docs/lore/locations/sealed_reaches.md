## Function

The island's nearest dungeon and its quietest dread: the upper wards of
[[highroof]], mortared shut generations ago, above the third reach
where no window opens. A vertical ruin threaded through the city's own
body — exploration that goes *up*.

## Canon summary

The Reaches were the citadel's finest wards once — highest, lightest,
closest to the old sky. They were sealed in the Coming's first years,
and the mortar work reads wrong to every mason who studies it: seams
struck from within, scaffold-holes on the inner faces, doors bricked
with their hinges left on the outside. The official record says the
wards were closed against the Above. The crown's standing purse for
reports of sounds inside says the record is incomplete.

## Visible traits

From the city below: blind limewashed faces, window-scars in tidy
rows. At the boundary galleries: bricked arches, hinge-pins bright
where curious hands polish them, warning-marks in an older signage
style. Cold drafts that move against the mountain's normal breath.

## Systems impact

The boundary galleries are patrolled and the seals are law — breaking
one is the Highroof's gravest civic crime short of unroofing a
neighbor. The crown's report-purse creates a small economy of listeners,
liars, and the occasional honest witness (factions pass decides who
audits it).

## Relationships

The crown of [[highroof]], above the [[undersun_galleries]]' reach,
neighbor to nothing — the sealed wards touch open sky on every outer
face. What sealed them ties to the Coming (timeline pass).

## Quest hooks

- A listener's report vanished along with the listener (see
  [[highroof]]) — the last page of their ledger survives.
- A mason's guild examination piece is a perfect copy of the Reaches'
  inside-out mortar seam; the examiner wants to know where the
  apprentice learned it.
- A seal has begun weeping warm air.

## Environmental storytelling

Hinges without doors. A child's chalk game abandoned mid-grid at the
boundary gallery's edge, redrawn fresh every few seasons by tradition.
The mortar seams' inward strike-marks, visible to any player who has
watched a mason work.
