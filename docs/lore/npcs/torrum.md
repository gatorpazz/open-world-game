## Function

The warden chain-giver (spec 010): the Gate-captain of [[lastdoor]]'s
post, voice of the standing request, and the door `for_the_tallies`
opens through. He carries the captaincy ruling — a practical enforcer
holding an instrument crisis he cannot enforce his way out of.

## Canon summary

Torrum rose on tally-service the ordinary way: gate watches, witnessed
crossings, a cord heavy with service knots and light on ceremony. He
holds the Lastdoor captaincy as a tool, not a pulpit — and the
[[the_untaken_arrival]] jammed the tool. His request to the stranger
is operational, said plainly and repeated without embarrassment: the
[[bait_square]] prices every crossing out of Lastdoor, the square
cannot price what it cannot read, so stand, under witness, for the
tallies. The Doctrinists call his request a rite wearing work clothes;
the Tally-keepers call it malpractice without new instruments; Torrum
calls it the day's work. (Ruled: spec 010 Phase 1 session — a Gate-captain
holds the captaincy.)

## Visible traits

Grey working wraps gone pale at the shoulders; a tally-cord whose
knots crowd the working end. Chalk on the heel of his right hand, most
hours. He stands gate-side of his own counter, never behind it, and
answers questions in the order they will matter.

## Systems impact

Gate law's local face: his word opens and closes the Last Gate, seals
crossings, and licenses witness. The chain runs on his offers — the
standing request, and on branch A's close, the join
(`joined_the_wardens`). If refused, the offer cools but does not die
(ruled: refusal defers).

## Relationships

Captain of the [[wardens]]' Lastdoor post; [[underfirst]] by descent,
roll-speech by habit. [[bryony]] is his Tally-keeper-leaning second
and his loyal opposition. The [[licensed_crews]] are his permanent
budget war made of neighbors; [[wester]] is the boss he argues with
by appointment.

## Quest hooks

- The captain's standing request (canon, [[the_untaken_arrival]]):
  return to the square under witness, for the tallies — half the town
  calls it science, half trial, half bait, and the halves don't sum.
- The budget one roof short (canon, [[wardens]]): the rescue crossing
  the tables can't afford lands on his desk in front of the player.
- The two-versions tally-log: both initials are from his post, and he
  has not asked which reading he wants to be true.

## Environmental storytelling

His notch-stick by the post door, worn smooth, counting something the
tables have no column for. The retired cords on the post's hooks —
his own future, hanging in rows.
