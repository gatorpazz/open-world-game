## Function

The island's portable luxury and its most legible gift: a scrimshaw
token in the hand says sea, grief, and money at once. As an item it is
a valuable with provenance — worth beads anywhere, worth more than
beads when given.

## Canon summary

Tokens are carved from loose fragments of the [[leviathan_ribs]] —
never cut from the standing bone — in the port trade founded on what
the beast left behind ([[the_beaching]]). The earliest pieces were
carved with a tenderness the trade later lost, and old tokens carry
old-family weight. They are Tidegate's signature luxury and its most
smuggled good, riding the [[tide_road]] past toll-books that admit
fewer of them than exist; fishers leave them at the third rib going
out, and mourners give them where words would be too plain.

## Visible traits

Grey-gold bone with lamp-oil patina; motifs of lanes, tallies, ribs,
and birds nobody living has seen. Wear-polish where a thumb rides.
The older the piece, the softer the carving — and the harder the
bargaining.

## Systems impact

A stable valuable: sells for beads at any market, carries a premium
at [[tidegate]] and a smuggler's discount everywhere else. Giving one
is a social act the island reads — luck to a crew, grief to a family,
apology to a Lastlight house. Warden manifests count them; the counts
are famously wrong.

## Relationships

Carved from the [[leviathan_ribs]] at [[tidegate]]; trade and
smuggling shadow priced by [[the_shares]]; a [[lastlight]] signature
good, left at shrines and worn on cords; moves through [[lastdoor]]
in crew pockets more often than in manifests.

## Quest hooks

- A glut of fresh-carved tokens is moving through Lastdoor — fresh
  bone, not rib-fall — and anyone who knows the trade knows what a
  second source would have to mean.

## Environmental storytelling

Tokens on cords at net-lofts and stall-shrines. The third rib's
touch-polish, ringed with left offerings. A pawn-shelf where one
token sits apart, older than the rest, never priced.
