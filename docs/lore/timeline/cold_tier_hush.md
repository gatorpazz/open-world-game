## Function

The island's small-scale mystery: a human-sized tragedy against the
epic ones, giving [[embershare]] its held breath and the writing its
proof that hushes come in parish sizes too. A quest-scale secret by
design.

## Canon summary

Within living memory, the highest tier of [[embershare]]'s terraces —
homes, ovens, a working stair of families — emptied in one season.
The parish recorded reassignments, not causes; the families were
resettled without ceremony across three settlements; and the tier was
neither reoccupied nor sealed. It is swept. The sweeping is on no
rota, funded by no line, done by no one who will say so, and the
parish loses the paperwork of anyone who petitions to move back in.

## Visible traits

Today: the cold tier's swept floors and ajar doors; warm air rising
past it untouched; the resettled families' particular silence at
festival times; the petitioners' lost paperwork ([[embershare]]'s
current scandal). The phrase "a swept floor," said quietly, for
matters closed without explanation.

## Systems impact

A standing exception inside parish law: a tier held empty against all
housing pressure, in the settlement least able to afford it. Whoever
funds the sweeping funds it invisibly — a thread the economy pass left
deliberately unpulled ([[the_shares]] notes the adjacency and reserves
it); faction quests can pull it.

## Relationships

Within [[embershare]]; the parish that hushed it takes shape in the
factions pass. The cause is reserved — deliberately quest-sized,
not world-sized.

## Quest hooks

- One resettled family kept their old tier key, oiled and worn from
  handling. They have never gone back. They cannot say why they keep
  it oiled.

## Environmental storytelling

Sweep-lines in dust that should be thicker. The tier's ovens, their
blessing-marks washed off — the only unblessed ovens on the island.
(On the working terraces below, one oven's mark goes unrefreshed — a
smaller, separate silence.) A child's height-marks inside one doorway,
stopping mid-year.

## Open questions

- What emptied the tier, who sweeps it, and why does the parish
  protect the silence? (Reserved as a quest secret — smaller than the
  sky, closer than the deep.)
