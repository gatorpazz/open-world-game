## Function

Supporting power: the civic-religious utility. Not a joinable guild —
the parishes are where the player eats, tithes, and is watched. Their
hospitality is the island's warmest surveillance.

## Canon summary

The Vent-Parishes are one institution with a chapter at every tended
vent, [[embershare]]'s great chapter first among them. They apportion
heat as commons, bless the ovens, keep the [[bellworks]]' founding
rights, and hold that every flame is attention — the Flame's, the
aspected orders teach ([[the_workings]]), though parish speech stays
carefully vague at table all the same. Their
public creed is hospitality: *no cold table under our roof.* Their
private truth is the [[cold_tier_hush]] — the emptied tier is a parish
matter, the sweeping is parish-funded through no ledger line, and
chapter discipline protects the silence absolutely.

## Visible traits

Blessing-marks, oven-liturgy, apportionment cords hung at vent-mouths.
Chapter hands in ash-grey with a single bright thread — the one
Underfirst institution that wears any color at all. Guest-portions
served unasked to strangers, and the server always remembers your
face.

## Systems impact

Bread, heat, and bells: the parishes' apportionment is the island's
base subsistence layer, and their tithe-collectors meet every salvage
manifest at [[lastdoor]]'s gate. Stall patronage on the [[ember_road]]
is chapter-granted. Refusing parish hospitality is legal and noted.

## Relationships

Chapters at every vent; the [[bellworks]] rite is theirs; frictions
with the [[licensed_crews]] over tithe-assessment and with the
deed-holders of [[undersun_galleries]] over the commons-vs-deed
divide. Toward [[the_untaken_arrival]]: the only power not courting
the player — the parishes feed and observe, and their patience is its
own message.

## Quest hooks

- A chapter's apportionment cord was re-knotted overnight — one
  household's heat halved, no record, no appeal, and the chapter hand
  who did it wears the bright thread of a different vent.
- The tithe-assessors' quarantine row (see [[licensed_crews]]) — the
  always-empty shelf is emptied by someone with a parish key.
- A stranger's guest-portion arrives with a question folded under the
  bowl: the parishes' first direct word to the player, and it is not
  a welcome.

## Environmental storytelling

Apportionment cords' knot-ledgers. The swept cold tier above
[[embershare]] and the washed-off blessing-marks. Guest-bowls with a
maker's mark from a chapter three vents away — the umbrella is one
body, and it circulates.
