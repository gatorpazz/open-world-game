## Function

The island's chitin at its source (spec 024): a slow, armored
gallery-scuttler that gives the player a tanky melee fight and drops raw
chitin — the wrights' unworked stock. Grounds where chitin-wrighting's
material comes from.

## Canon summary

Chitin-crawlers are low, plated grazers of the unlit galleries, their
backs a fitted amber-black shell oiled to the sheen the wrights prize. The
[[chitin_wrights]] work wright-offcut chitin and the island's scout chitin
into helm, shield, bow-lath, gates, and bridge-trusses; the crawler is the
living stock those offcuts are cut from. Slow and near-sighted, it ignores
the covers until cornered, then turns its armored bulk and bites.
