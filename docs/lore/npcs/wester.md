## Function

The licensed_crews' recruiting voice (spec 010): boss of the Lastlight
crew whose licensed book [[suddel]] keeps, holder of the roofed-cache
rumor, and the mouth of `joined_the_licensed_crews` — the crews' offer
to the one walker the sky doesn't price.

## Canon summary

Wester is a wind-named boss of the [[licensed_crews]], weathered the
way lane-rope is weathered, running salvage out of [[lastdoor]] into
the [[open_fields]] on shares arithmetic and casual understatement.
His crew is the standing Underfirst-Lastlight pairing: Lastlight backs
on the open ground, Suddel's Underfirst hand on the licensed book.
(Ruled, spec 010 roster session: Wester is the boss of Suddel's crew —
closing that entry's open question.) His courtship of the stranger is
the crews' reading of the arrival said as a budget: an untaken partner
rewrites every exposure line in the book. His opener is the roofed
cache — the Lastdoor rumor that is true, and older than any license.

## Visible traits

Sky-scarf worn even under roof; rope-burned palms; the crew's chalk
marks made with his thumb, not a stick. He counts shares aloud in
front of everyone, Lastlight-fashion — the arithmetic is the honesty.

## Systems impact

Recruitment: his partnership offer stages `joined_the_licensed_crews`
through the `the_roofed_cache` mini-quest (ruled). His crew's runs set
half the warehouse row's stock; his standing grievance with the
[[wardens]]' budget ceilings is [[torrum]]'s standing appointment.

## Relationships

Boss of a licensed crew of the [[licensed_crews]]; [[lastlight]] by
descent and volume. [[suddel]] keeps his licensed book — and takes
care never to see the other one. Argues budgets with [[torrum]];
loses crew to the open some seasons, and walks the funeral line
[[dossun]] keeps.

## Quest hooks

- The roofed cache (canon, [[licensed_crews]]): the Lastdoor rumor is
  true — and the cache's roof predates the crews. Wester offers the
  secret as a share. (Ruled, spec 010: the cache lies off the Scar
  route — the covered stretch between the gate-country and the
  [[drowned_stair]], where the [[open_fields]] press closest.)
- The stranded crew / budget one roof short (canon, braided with the
  wardens' seed): Wester's people, Torrum's tables, the town knowing
  both numbers.
- The other ledger (canon, [[suddel]]): the book the best crews keep,
  which Suddel has never seen. Wester decides what goes in it — and
  someone has asked Suddel to carry one number across.

## Environmental storytelling

The out-crew chalk on Suddel's shutter is Wester's system in Suddel's
hand. A coil of lane-rope on his shoulder even at the counter — a boss
who still walks. One share-token on his string gone black: carried for
someone who no longer collects.
