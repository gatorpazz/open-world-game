## Function

Joinable guild one: the soldiery-and-science verb set (measure, patrol,
license, witness, test) and the living edge of shelter-vs-stagnation —
defend the roof, or calcify under it. Their courtship of the player is
the game's opening pressure: the untaken arrival is their
constitutional crisis.

## Canon summary

The Wardens keep [[warden_doctrine]]: the tables, tallies, and licenses
that price every hour of open sky. They are a precedent-order — the
[[crown_compact]] recognized them but nobody commands them; they answer
to the doctrine itself, promotion runs on tally-service, and their
authority is the fact that the tables have always held. Their public
creed is exactly that: *the tables hold.* Their private truth is
double: the tables carry unpublished exceptions the captains circulate
by hand, and the doctrine's wooden baseline was planed and re-cut by
someone, long ago, and the order has never found out by whom.

## Visible traits

Chitin-plated blinds and gate-posts; chalk, notch, and mirror-rod;
grey working wraps with the tally-cord worn at the shoulder — knots
for years of service, beads for witnessed crossings. Underfirst and
Lastlight serve in near-equal numbers: the one institution the cut
runs through quietly, because the sky prices both descents the same.

## Systems impact

Licenses, budgets, gate law, and the [[bait_square]]'s instrument
authority. When wardens close a gate, it is closed; when their tables
and a crew's ledger disagree, the island's courts take the tables. The
report-purse of [[highroof]] galls them — a second, sealed doctrine
implies theirs is not the whole arithmetic. Joinable (spec 010, ruled):
membership in one guild bars none of the others — every guild wants
first claim on the untaken outsider, none has veto.

## Relationships

Keep the squares at [[lastdoor]]; police [[open_fields]] entry; argue
jurisdiction weekly with [[tidegate]]'s net-bosses at the cave mouth;
cold with the [[reciters]] (every death declaration breaks both
orders' books); permanent budget war with the [[licensed_crews]]. The
[[the_untaken_arrival]] is their open wound: doctrine has no higher
authority to appeal to, so the anomaly is theirs to resolve or be
broken by.

## Quest hooks

- The moth test: the post's version of the question half the island is
  quietly testing — the player invited to stand near a cage "for the
  tallies," and the answers never agree.
- The jumper's file: a grief-jumper rose from a sister court following
  a kept name; the tri-key has no procedure for a license nobody asked
  for, and the wardens hold the budget key — file it as exposure and
  the tables absorb grief, refuse and the tables admit a hole.
- The seal before the seal: a warehouse is cornering fish-oil ahead of
  a declaration that hasn't happened — someone reads the tallies
  before the wardens speak, or a warden speaks early.
- The budget one roof short: a stranded crew's rescue crossing costs
  more than the town holds this close to seal — the tables' arithmetic
  against living neighbors, and the town knows both numbers.
- The captain's standing request: the player returns to the square
  under witness — science, trial, and bait in one act, and the guild
  splits on which it is.
- An unpublished exception surfaces in a dead captain's hand-copied
  tables — and it describes a case older captains have clearly seen
  before.
- The planed baseline: a Doctrinist wants the original tally-post's
  planed layer read by lamplight and rubbing — heresy to the
  Tally-keepers, overdue truth to the Gate-captains.

## Environmental storytelling

Tally-cords retired on hooks in every warden post, oldest knots gone
grey. The apprentice wall of copied doctrine, error-corrected in a
second hand. A gate-captain's private notch-stick, worn smooth,
counting something the tables have no column for.
