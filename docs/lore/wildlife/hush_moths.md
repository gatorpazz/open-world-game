## Function

The art's camp-follower and the island's living instrument: hush-moths
make working visible, which makes it priceable — a species whose whole
niche is one rule of [[the_workings]]. Ambient omen in play, and the
candidate detection tell for spec 008: casting near a cage is casting
under witness.

## Canon summary

Hush-moths are pale, soft-winged moths of the roofed dark that gather
where regard has just been spent: a finished working, an overcast
stranger, the last echo of an unwrapped bell. They crowd lamps — every
flame is attention — and lamp-keepers curse them by name; blessing is
said to thin them at a hearth, and the parishes neither confirm it nor
charge less for it. Wardens cage them as living detectors: a caged
moth stirring means something worked nearby, and a moth-fall — the
sudden soft descent of a whole cloud — will stop a market
mid-sentence. "The moths came" is the island's phrase for a working
found out.

## Visible traits

Palm-small, grey-white, wings dusted like fungal velvet; flight
utterly silent. Spoor: pale wing-dust filmed over lamp-hoods and
lintels. They rise in numbers nobody counts and settle all at once —
the held-breath hush of a moth-fall is how most children learn the
word.

## Systems impact

Detection: caged moths hang at warden posts, gates, and shaft-mouths;
a working near one is a working witnessed (spec 008 tell). Economy:
moth-catchers sell to the wardens by the cage, and a good
catching-ground is guarded knowledge. Travel: a moth-fall on a
crossing means notice has pooled somewhere close — crews go to cover
first and argue after.

## Relationships

Creatures of [[the_workings]]' one big rule; caged by the [[wardens]];
cursed at every lamp in [[undervault]]. Thickest where regard runs
rich — under the [[undersun_galleries]]' deep lenses, and in the
[[bellworks]]' long gallery, where apprentices brush them from the
bronze between strikes.

## Quest hooks

- Every caged moth at a warden post died in the same night. The post
  wants to know what, that close, worked that hard.
- A moth-catcher is selling catch too fresh for any known ground — and
  will not name where they hang their cages.
- Whether the moths gather on the untaken stranger is a question half
  the island has quietly arranged to test. The answers do not agree.

## Environmental storytelling

Wing-dust wiped from lamp-hoods in the same motion as lighting them.
An empty cage at a warden post, door open, hung facing the
[[open_fields]] — bait or vigil, depending who you ask. Children's
chalk game of moth-fall: everyone drops at once, and the last to move
wins.
