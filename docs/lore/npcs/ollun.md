## Function

The chain's living symptom: the rank-and-file reciter whose rotation
carries the removed name. A discipline case to the order, a witness to
the player, and the human stake in the expose-or-absolve choice.

## Canon summary

Ollun is a young Walker of the circuit, raised to the discipline —
level voice, forbidden weeping, nine days of names. Since the night
the mortar dried in the [[grief_court]]'s wall, Ollun's rotation has
carried one name that stands on no tablet, spoken without breaking
stride. Discipline says stop; the Column-Readers say *write it down
first*; Ollun keeps walking and keeps saying it. The name is the
removed one — the wall lost it and the voice did not (ruled, quests
pass). How it reached Ollun's rotation, and what Ollun understands
about why, stays with the chain.

## Visible traits

Plain wraps a size large, cinched with doubled knots; a staff whose
nine finger-stops are still sharp-edged and bright. Young hands, old
voice — the discipline sits on Ollun like borrowed formality. Found
walking the wall at set hours, and afterward at the court's edge,
counting pebbles nobody asked anyone to count.

## Systems impact

None yet beyond the rite: Ollun holds no counter, no gate, no price.
What Ollun holds is the name — and in a system where derived quest
state is journal entries, Ollun's recitation is the chain's ticking
clock: every circuit that carries the extra name is another chance the
inner circle stops pretending not to hear it.

## Relationships

A Walker of the [[reciters]]; [[underfirst]] by descent, parish-raised.
Walks the [[grief_court]] circuit; sponsored into the rite young.
[[kellom]] is discipline's face to Ollun, and possibly its exception.

## Quest hooks

- The extra name: stop Ollun, write the name down first, or find out
  where a Walker's rotation learns a name the wall no longer teaches.
- Ollun's discipline hearing is the chain's ignored-path consequence —
  a case the order can only win by explaining what it cannot say.
- The waxed staff (see [[reciters]], braided with the chain): Ollun
  noticed it first — the circuit closes where it hangs — and has not
  told the copy-hall.

## Environmental storytelling

Wax shavings in Ollun's wrap-hem — someone has been teaching staff
maintenance, or censure. A pebble row at the court's edge, nine and
one apart. The rotation-slate with one line in a younger hand.
