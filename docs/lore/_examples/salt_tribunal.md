## Function

Local ruling power and early-game gatekeeper faction of [[marshgate_weir]].
Excellent for lawful-but-morally-uneasy quests: missions that ask the
player to preserve stability while glimpsing the human cost of that
stability.

## Canon summary

A civic-religious water authority whose public creed is "Water remembers
theft. Salt remembers debt." Its legal authority rests on old flood law,
but its real leverage is ledger secrecy, floodgate control, and a ritual
monopoly over purification seals. It perpetuates scarcity myths because
apparent indispensability is safer than democratic access.

## Visible traits

Stacked salt-white cords, mirrored pendants, slate tablets, flood masks,
bronze clappers, square-shouldered robes built to stay dry above the knee.
Speech is measured, ceremonial, and exact with nouns — even accusations are
phrased as clerical corrections.

## Systems impact

Controls gate schedules and water rations in [[marshgate_weir]]; licenses
ferrymen; restricts certain magic in the upper sluice ward. Internal
subgroups pull in different directions: the Ledger Choir (accountants,
seal-keepers, auditors), the Bell Wardens (inspectors, flood marshals, gate
troops), and the Quiet Basin (archivists who study pre-flood maps and know
the current system is corrupted).

## Relationships

Allied with grain import factors. In cold detente with marsh temple houses.
Openly hostile to unauthorized ferrymen and siphon gangs. Vulnerable to
blackmail from a noble family whose private reservoirs were never declared.

## Quest hooks

- Audit a district ledger the Ledger Choir does not want opened.
- Escort a purification seal past saboteurs who want the tithe to fail.
- Empower the Quiet Basin against the Bell Wardens — or expose both.

## Environmental storytelling

Quota slabs and doorway salt marks maintained by wardens. Sealed cisterns
with mirrored inspection rods. A drowned archive the Quiet Basin quietly
dredges by night.

## Open questions

- The player's faction arc: entrench the Tribunal, reform it through audit
  and exposure, or split it?
- Recruitment verbs in play: audit, escort, confiscate, mediate, sanitize,
  witness.
