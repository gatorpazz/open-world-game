The hub of the lore vault (spec 011) and the reachability root: every live
entry must be reachable from here by `[[id]]` links. Product pillars live in

derived details remain draft until ratified.

## Core promise

**The fantasy:** you are the outsider a hidden world learns to trust —
found impossibly untaken in a bait square of the roofed island of
[[undervault]], climbing its parishes and guilds until the factions ask
you the only question that matters: does the island keep hiding from
the sky, or fight for it?

**The twist that is law:** [[the_taking]] — stand under open sky and the
hives will come for you; levitation is the predator's own art, stolen.
Everything bends around the roof.

**Emotional palette:** oppressive · sacred · luminous · patient · resolute.
Lamplight and liturgy pressed under an enormous silence; the roofed world
is one continuous temple.

**Forbidden clichés:**

- **No mindless swarm.** The ants are never just bugs to squash; the
  Taking has a logic — alien, perhaps never fully revealed, but present.
- **No misery-porn underground.** The roofed world is lived-in, luminous,
  occasionally funny. A district that is only grim is wrong.
- **No generic-medieval default.** Materials, dress, law, and idiom derive
  from chitin, fungus, stone, rope, and lava-glass. If it could sit
  unchanged in a stock castle game, redo it.

## Rules of the world

- **Cosmology:** the sky is not heaven — it is held territory. Warmth and
  light rise from below, from the lava veins that serve as the caverns'
  under-sun (infrastructure and mystery both; divinity disputed by
  design — [[the_workings]]).
- **The law:** [[the_taking]] — the rule of record for the roofed world.
- **Magic:** real, aspected, and administered by religious orders of the
  pantheon — the rule of record is [[the_workings]] (M2 magic pass,
  feeds spec 008); levitation exists and is the most precious and
  contested art, stolen by watching and tri-key licensed.
- **Technology:** rope, stone, fungus-craft, chitin-wrighting, lava-glass;
  timber and paper are closed stocks — pre-Coming beams, surface
  salvage, and rag-fungus pulp, with true daylight paper an archival
  treasure; heat and light are governed utilities — deeded in the
  Highroof, held as parish commons in Embershare; the rivalry between
  the two regimes is deliberate and unresolved. Gear follows the same
  stocks (ruled, spec 014): blades are recast bell-bronze salvage,
  helm, shield, and bow-lath are wright-offcut chitin, light armor is
  oil-dark hound-hide — the island has no native iron or steel.
- **Economy:** [[the_shares]] — bead-glass cash under three credit
  layers; heat-capped growth; toll, tithe, and the sea's share; the
  warden-declared seal of high-sky season.
- **Death, travel, communication:** the honored dead go down into stone —
  only the Taken go up. Travel is roofed galleries, covered under-roads,
  and priced open sprints; no open water, no unshafted flight. News moves
  by bell-code, shaft-echo, and runners.
- **Time:** [[telling_time]] — no sun to read, so the day is a round of
  bells and the long count is the nine-day circuit of the grief-rite.

## Historical spine

Three eras (the source of truth for `VALID_ERAS`), grief-shaped: the world
lost, the world wounded, the world enduring. Clerks date in Roll-years
since the Coming's first winter (format: "Roll 214" — the current year
number is deliberately unfixed); folk date by generations and events
("two lives after the Still Year," "the year the beast came ashore"). The deep masonry below [[the_scar]] belongs to no era — it is
older than the spine and stays undated on purpose.

## Peoples & powers

Vaultfolk is the collective word for all islanders (confirmed, cultures
pass); beneath it run two descents cut by the Coming itself: the
[[underfirst]], first under the roof, people of the count and the
hearth-name; and the [[lastlight]], who held the surface longest,
people of the word-hoard and the song. The pantheon is real and its
orders are tied to aspects of magic; the reciters' office descends from
the Daylight priesthood, serving the grief-rite as penance
([[the_still_year]]); parishes tend the lava vents; guilds run the
roofed roads, the salvage trade, and the fisheries; a deliberately weak
crown arbitrates ([[crown_compact]]). The powers (factions pass): the
three joinable guilds — [[wardens]], [[reciters]], [[licensed_crews]] —
and the supporting powers [[vent_parishes]], [[chitin_wrights]], and
[[the_crown]]. The fight-for-the-sky pole of the Core promise lives at
the reciters' radical edge: the Homebringers, the heresy that reads
*kept, not dead* as a debt — open the sky, bring the Taken home. The
wildlife of the dark: [[hush_moths]], [[scar_hound]], [[vault_rat]].

## Content grammar

- **Dialogue tone defaults:** measured · euphemistic · exact · warm-dry ·
  unhurried. People under-name the danger: the hives are "the Above," the
  Taken are "kept," the surface is "the open." Saying the plain words
  reads as transgressive or intimate.
- **Naming rules (the translation convention, ruled in the cultures
  pass):** folk speech renders in transparent English — [[underfirst]]
  names say what a thing *is* (closed compounds: Highroof, Embershare;
  roll-names + hearth-names), [[lastlight]] names say what it *was like
  in the light* (open bright syllables; the daylight word-hoard) — and
  **only the [[high_register|High Register]] is untranslated**: the old formal tongue of
  the unreadable columns, the named bells, seal-texts, and the
  reciters' rite forms. Its vocabulary is reserved; its flavor is
  formulaic pairs, never explained in dialogue. No real-language
  copies; no apostrophe gibberish.
- **Material logic:** if an object, garment, or building can't say *rope,
  stone, fungus, chitin, or lava-glass*, it must justify itself.
- **Forbidden tropes:** the Core promise bans, applied at review; hooks
  and rumors must serve at least one core tension (depth vs. sky · shelter
  vs. stagnation · grief vs. hope).
- **Plain-meaning anchor (ruled, spec 010 playthrough):** every dialogue
  response lands its plain meaning — who, what, what the player can do —
  on first read; euphemism and metaphor season a line, never carry it
  alone. Under-naming stays; riddling as the whole line does not.
- **Lore-text markup:** cross-reference with flat `[[id]]` wikilinks only;
  quote example dialogue inside code fences so it never links.

## Player-facing surfaces

- **The `surface` contract:** every reviewed entry declares where it
  reaches the player — dialogue · journal · environment · rumor · quest —
  or `none` (legal, but audited: canon that never surfaces is rot).
- **Spoiler policy:** `high` is reserved for the sky's logic — what the
  Taking is *for*, what the hives hold, what came back down. High-spoiler
  facts stay out of ambient dialogue and out of canon packets unless the
  director overrides. `low` covers politics and secrets; `none` is common
  knowledge under the roof.
