## Function

The origin of chitin-wrighting and the [[chitin_bridge]] — and the
island's most disciplined act of collective not-asking. The event that
gave the roofed world its second great material and its habit of
declining to discuss where it came from.

## Canon summary

In one season of the roofed years, felled scouts became plentiful:
carapaces enough to truss a bridge, plate a gate, and mount a port's
cranes, gathered — the record says only *gathered* — and worked by
the first chitin-wrights. No telling recited in public says why the
sky's own armor came within reach that year, and the wrights'
founding charter opens with the line their guild still swears by:
*the shell serves; the shell is not asked.* The bridge went up across
[[the_scar]] within the Shell Year's decade, and the toll has run
since.

## Visible traits

Today: every chitin structure on the island — bridge, Last Gate
plating, [[tidegate]]'s cranes, warden blinds. The wrights' oath of
not-asking, taught as craft-discipline. The stranger's mistake of
asking about the Shell Year in a tavern, and the specific quality of
the pause that follows.

## Systems impact

Chitin is the island's second closed material economy (with
[[first_bells]]' bronze): a fixed stock, maintained not replenished,
its allocation an old political settlement nobody wants reopened. The
bridge toll's "upkeep" justification survives on the fact that the
chitin has never needed any.

## Relationships

Raises the [[chitin_bridge]]; plates [[lastdoor]]'s gate; founds the
wrights (factions pass). What felled the scouts — battle, bargain, or
harvest — is reserved (spoiler: high; [[chitin_bridge]] keeps the
question).

## Quest hooks

- The wrights' founding charter has a second page, sewn shut by the

  about it exactly once.

## Environmental storytelling

Carapace plates with matching fracture-patterns spread across
structures a road apart — pieces of the same individuals, worked into
the world's furniture. The one newer plate at the bridge's midpoint
that collects no story.
