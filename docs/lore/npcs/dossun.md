## Function

The reciters' Lastdoor face (spec 010): keeper of the gate shrine of
borrowed grief-tablets, the courtship voice for "a reading," and the
local anchor the re-homed chain beats visit — far from the copy-hall,
close to the door people don't come back through.

## Canon summary

Dossun is an old gate-post reciter, parish-raised and roll-named,
serving where the order's law frays: [[lastdoor]], whose dead are
mostly not Taken but *lost* — a word tablet law has no clean
procedure for. He keeps the crew shrine of borrowed grief-tablets by
the gate (canon, [[lastdoor]]), tends names the wall at [[grief_court]]
does not hold, and stands as near the funeral lines as the order's law
lets him — how near is the standing question. His courtship
of the stranger is the order's reading said gently: stand at circuit's
end and be read — not because the order knows what you are, but
because the roll is how the island keeps what it cannot explain.
(Ruled, spec 010: the gate shrine grows into a modest interior; the
skipped-name beats re-home there when Kellom and Ollun come down to
the gate.)

## Visible traits

Wraps mended in a parish hand; a staff whose finger-stops are worn in
the wrong places — gate-line rites, not the court circuit. He recites
in the level discipline but greets in plain roll-speech, and the
switch is itself a kind of kindness.

## Systems impact

Keeper of the gate shrine (the re-homed chain's Lastdoor geography).
His word carries the reciters' courtship beat; the reading itself, and
novice standing, remain [[kellom]]'s to grant.

## Relationships

A gate-post reciter of the [[reciters]]; [[underfirst]] by descent.
Keeps the crew shrine's borrowed tablets for the [[licensed_crews]] —
[[wester]]'s blacked share-token has a twin here. Hosts [[kellom]] and
[[ollun]] when the circuit business comes down the [[last_road]].
Cool, correct relations with [[torrum]]'s post across the way — every
declaration of death breaks both orders' books.

## Quest hooks

- The funeral line (canon, [[reciters]]): a salvager dead beyond the
  covers — a Lastlight funeral the order won't walk, at the gate
  post's door.
- The borrowed tablets: every tablet in his shrine is on loan from the
  wall's law — and someone at the court has begun asking for them
  back.
- The blacked token's twin: [[wester]] carries a share-token gone
  black; its twin hangs in Dossun's case. Who the pair mourns, neither
  man will say first.

## Environmental storytelling

Borrowed grief-tablets in a chitin-plated case, each with its lending
mark. A funeral-line rope coiled by the door, waxed against weather
the roof never brings. Nine pebbles and a gap on the shrine sill —
someone else's habit, learned.
