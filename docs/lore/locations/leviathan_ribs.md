## Function

Tidegate's gate and standing question: the arched remains of the
leviathan that beached itself generations ago, now fused into the
port's architecture. Scale, history, and unease in one structure — the
sea's counterweight to the sky's hives.

## Canon summary

The beast came ashore in living memory's grandparents' time: drove
itself against the cliff at [[tidegate]]'s cave mouth and died over
nine days, during which the port fed it, sang to it, or hid from it,
depending on whose grandmother tells it. Its ribs were left where they
settled and the port grew through them — crane-mounts bolted to bone,
lamp-hooks in the vertebrae, the tally-carvings of every season since.
Fishers touch the third rib going out and the first coming home. What
it fled is not discussed with strangers.

## Visible traits

Bone arches higher than any roofed hall, grey-gold with lamp-oil
patina. Tally-carvings crowding the reachable spans. Crane-ropes
creaking against mounts of blackened bone and chitin. Between the ribs: the
truce-line lanes and the cold bright water.

## Systems impact

The ribs are Tidegate's fixed infrastructure — cranes, gantries,
lamp-mounts; replacing them is unthinkable in both senses. Scrimshaw
from loose fragments is the port's signature luxury and its most
smuggled good ([[tide_road]]). The nine-days story is the port's civic
identity and its argument with itself.

## Relationships

The mouth of [[tidegate]]; kin-object to every chitin structure on the
island (the other salvaged giant); the beaching sits early in the
timeline pass's work. The rumored second skeleton would change the
scrimshaw trade and the story both.

## Quest hooks

- The second skeleton (see [[tidegate]]): a scrimshaw glut traces to a
  cove the truce-lines do not cover.
- The ninth-day accounts conflict in one loud detail — whether the
  beast was singing back. A collector pays for grandmothers' tellings,
  verbatim, and someone is editing them.
- A tally-carving from the beaching year was chiseled out last month.
  The gap is exactly one season wide.

## Environmental storytelling

The touch-polished third rib. Lamp-soot shadows of vanished gantries —
the port has shrunk. A vertebra worked into the drowned shrine at the
low-tide mark, facing out to sea.
