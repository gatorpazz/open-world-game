## Function

A slow, armored gallery-scuttler (spec 024) that gives the player a tanky
melee fight and drops a sellable plate. Grounds `raw_chitin` as a *lesser
native* stock so the [[shell_year]]'s fixed carapace economy stays closed:
crawler plate is cheap, common, and asked after freely; the wrights' shell
is none of those things.

## Canon summary

Chitin-crawlers are low, plated grazers of the unlit galleries, their
backs a fitted shell of dull grey-brown plate that takes oil badly and
never comes to the amber-black gloss of the [[chitin_bridge]]. Crawler
plate is a lesser native stock: soft enough to cut with a crew knife,
bought by the plate by any crew, good for patching a wrap, sheathing a
crate edge, or lining a lamp niche. The [[chitin_wrights]] refuse it for
structure and for wright-work — the shell they swear by came once, in the
[[shell_year]], and is not asked after; crawler plate is asked after
freely, and that is the whole difference. Slow and near-sighted, a crawler
ignores the covers until cornered, then turns its armored bulk and bites.
