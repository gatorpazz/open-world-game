## Function

The Reciters' face for spec 006: sponsor of the player's novice
standing, petitioner of the reading, and the quest-giver the
removed-tablet chain runs through. A gatekeeper twice over — to the
order, and to the High Register right of reading.

## Canon summary

Kellom is a Column-Reader grown old in the copy-hall, one of the few
who hold the sanctioned right to the [[first_roof]]'s oldest text. The
untaken arrival split his order three ways — hope, heresy, omen — and
Kellom is the omen party's patient center: he filed the petition to
have the player stand in the [[grief_court]] at circuit's end and be
read in the High Register. It is Kellom who offers the order's answer
to an unrolled outsider — novice standing under the rite's own roof,
no parish roll asked or offered. What he reads in the player, and what
he wants it to say, he has not told anyone.

## Visible traits

Plain wraps worn thin at the elbows from copy-hall tables; a walking
staff with nine finger-stops gone shallow with age. Level voice even
for a reciter. Found in the Highroof copy-hall between circuit hours,
and at the [[grief_court]]'s wall when the ninth day closes.

## Systems impact

Sponsor: his word grants the player novice standing (the `joined_*`
convention's first live door). Gate: sanctioned reading of High
Register text goes through the Column-Readers, which in practice means
through him. He teaches the count, the discipline, and — when the
tablet scandal forces it — what absolution is for.

## Relationships

A Column-Reader of the [[reciters]]; [[underfirst]] in descent and
register. Keeper-adjacent to the [[grief_court]]'s circuit; holds
reading rights at the [[first_roof]]. Sponsor to the player; the
Walker [[ollun]] is his discipline problem and his conscience,
in whichever order the chain settles.

## Quest hooks

- The omen-petition: Kellom wants the player read at circuit's end —
  and the order's three parties each want the reading to say a
  different thing.
- The removed tablet lands on his table: the order's law has no
  procedure, and Kellom's copy-hall is where procedure is written.
- The expose-or-absolve choice goes through him — whichever the player
  brings, Kellom must make it the order's answer.

## Environmental storytelling

A copy-hall table with the Still Year's page left open — not reading
it, keeping it company. His staff on the rack's end hook, stops
unwaxed. The petition's fair copy, recopied more than once.
