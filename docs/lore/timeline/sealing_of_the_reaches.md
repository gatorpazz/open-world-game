## Function

The island's institutional secret: the event whose official record and
physical evidence disagree, and the origin of the crown's strangest
standing expense. Gives the [[sealed_reaches]] their history and the
Highroof its quietest dread.

## Canon summary

In the Coming's years — after the Going-Under, before the roofed
order settled — the citadel's upper wards were closed and mortared.
The record, kept since the first roofed councils, is brief: *sealed
against the Above, by common assent, none remaining within.* The
mortar-work reads from the inside; the hinges stayed outside; the
record does not address this, and never has. The report-purse — the
crown's standing payment for accounts of sounds within — appears in
the ledgers within a generation of the sealing, uninterrupted since.

## Visible traits

Today: the blind upper faces of [[highroof]]; the bricked boundary
arches with their bright-polished hinge-pins; the listeners' small
economy; the masons' quiet professional consensus that nobody
discusses in guild examinations ([[sealed_reaches]]'s current scandal
profanes exactly this).

## Systems impact

Seal-law — the gravest civic crime short of unroofing a neighbor —
dates from this event and extends by analogy to every official seal
on the island: cisterns, ledgers, tombs. Break a seal, and this
event is the precedent you are tried under.

## Relationships

Seals the [[sealed_reaches]] within [[the_coming]]'s era; the
report-purse runs through [[highroof]]'s crown ledgers; what the
mortar means is reserved (spoiler: high).

## Quest hooks

- The first roofed council's assent-roll survives in an archive — with

## Environmental storytelling

The record's single sentence, displayed publicly and unexpanded for
generations — brevity as its own monument. Hinge-pins polished by
curious thumbs. The oldest ledger line of the report-purse: payment
rendered, *report withheld by order*.
