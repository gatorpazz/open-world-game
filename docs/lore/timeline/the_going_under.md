## Function

The founding of underground life: the season the survivors walled the
[[first_roof]] and the roofed world began. The origin of "under one
roof" as the island's word for safety, family, and law at once.

## Canon summary

Through the Coming's first year the survivors gathered into the great
natural vault beneath what would become [[highroof]], walling its
mouth with terrace-stone carried down under half-roofs by night. The
refuge season was hunger, tallow-light, and counting: who was in, who
was kept, who would go back up for seed and tools and whether they
would return. From its awning-frames and desperate order grew
everything — the wards above, the rites, the first roofed laws.

## Visible traits

Today: the [[first_roof]] shrine with its fossilized awnings and
threshold tallies. "Under one roof" said at weddings, treaties, and
truces. The reflex of counting heads at every door — a courtesy now,
a survival rite then.

## Systems impact

The legal fiction that all Vaultfolk share one roof descends from the
refuge season — it is why the crown can arbitrate at all, and why
exiling someone "from the roof" is the heaviest sentence the island
has (its use is quest-level; spec 006).

is clearly terrace-riser, hauled from the daylight world it used to
hold up. Tallow soot in strata, thickest at the bottom.
