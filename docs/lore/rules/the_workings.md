## Function

The magic rule-of-record (M2 magic pass, director decisions) and spec
008's design input: what working is, what it costs, who may do it, and
why the island's most precious art is also its most policed. It is
[[the_taking]] read from the other side — one law, two faces — and it
fixes the aspects, the licensing tiers, the tri-key, the feared
figures, and the island's impossible building.

## Canon summary

To work is to draw regard — the attention that flame, stone, and tide
pay to the world — and hold it, turn it, or spend it; the parish creed
that every flame is attention is the plain form of the same claim.
Three lawful aspects answer to the pantheon's orders under the
vent-parish umbrella — the Flame, the Stone, and the Tide, plain-named
in folk speech, their true names High Register and rite-only. The
worker's price is notice: a working makes the worker remarkable, spent
in the same currency the open sky prices, and to work too much is to
remain remarkable under any roof. Levitation stands apart — no aspect, no
god's art: stolen by watching the Takings themselves, an imitation of
the lift, and licensed by no single power. Whether the lava veins are
the Flame's living attention or deeded property is the island's
standing dispute; both are taught, both are pleaded, and neither has
ever won.

## Visible traits

Blessing-marks and hearth-charms wherever the roof holds — an oven
blessed, a bell quieted, a doorway hushed: the art at hearth level,
free and unlicensed. Lamps lidded and hooded, light pointing down or
in; nothing on the island is built to be seen from above or afar. Moth-cages hung at
warden posts and shaft-mouths ([[hush_moths]]). And in the licensed
shafts of [[highroof]], the rising itself: arms loose, head back, feet
together — the posture of the Taken, practiced as a rite and watched
from the tiers in silence.

## Systems impact

Tiers (spec 008's progression gate): hearth-charm — free custom,
universal, domestic · chartered craft — lay licenses under an order
(mirror-wrights and bell-founders charter under the Flame,
tablet-carvers under the Stone, salters and lane-ropers under the
Tide; the [[lastlight]] singers are unchartered and proudly so) ·
ordained aspect-work — the orders' own arts. Costs: every working
spends notice; doctrine's tables of time, sound, and light are the
folk arithmetic of regard, so a working in the open spends the
crossing's budget and the worker's notice both. The overcast — spent
past recovery, permanently remarkable — are feared as contagion and
sheltered as a question. Law: aspect-licenses are order business;
levitation runs on the tri-key — a parish blessing, a warden budget,
and the crown's file, each revocable alone, and one revoked key
grounds a levitator; rising without all three makes a jumper, and what
wardens do about jumpers is not discussed. Items: the orders teach that a
worked thing is a regarded thing — objects hold the attention given
them. It is the founders' old claim that this is why recast bells keep
their voices ([[first_bells]]); it is everyone's claim that lava-glass
holds the charge best, which is why lens and bead are glass; and the
wrights' claim that regard slides off shell — why blinds are chitin —
is folk-certain and unproven. And the beacon is the island's
impossible building: no lighthouse, no signal-fire, no lit spire — to
build a gathering of regard is to build an invitation, and the island
has never once doubted that the sky answers invitations.

## Relationships

Priced by [[the_taking]] and measured by the [[wardens]]; the three
orders stand under the [[vent_parishes]]' umbrella; the lava dispute
cuts sharpest through the [[undersun_galleries]]' deed-law; the
tri-key meets at [[highroof]], where the licensed shafts run and
[[the_crown]] keeps the file of who may rise; the [[bellworks]] casts
worked voices and wraps them; the [[grief_court]] keeps what the orders
call the island's one sanctioned gathering of regard — kept names
offered to the sky.
[[hush_moths]] follow the art wherever it is spent. The [[reciters]]
are no aspected order — the grief-rite is penance, not craft — and
both offices prefer that said plainly.

## Quest hooks

- A grief-jumper rose from a gate-town grief-court, following a kept
  name up. The wardens want it filed as exposure, the family as grief,
  the parish not at all — and the tri-key has no procedure for a
  license nobody asked for.
- A levitator still rises with one key revoked — blessing, budget, or
  file: which was pulled, who pulled it, and who profits from a
  three-way license with a dead letter in it?
- An overcast traveler has taken a room under a parish roof. The creed
  says no cold table; the neighbors count the moths on the eaves; and
  someone has quietly asked the wardens what a roof is worth with a
  beacon lodged under it.
- A steady light shows after dark in the [[open_fields]], visible from
  a gate-mouth. No crew signals that way — nothing built to be seen
  out there has ever been built twice.

## Environmental storytelling

Hush-knots on door-frames and lamp-hoods sold beside felt at every
market stall. Tier-benches at a shaft-mouth, polished by onlookers who
attend the rising the way other towns attend funerals. A tri-key
license on its cord: a chitin tag drilled for three seals, one hole
conspicuously empty. A warden post's moth-cage, wiped of wing-dust in
the same motion as the morning lamp-lighting.

  ("Lava-Glass Grit", a mana reagent). ~~The give-back — whether stored
  regard can be released back to a person~~ — **ruled (spec 023): a
  worked thing may give its regard back.** A bound working is a stored
