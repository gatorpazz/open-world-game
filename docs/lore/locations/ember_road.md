## Function

The covered spoke between [[highroof]] and [[embershare]]: staple food
and worked lava-glass one way, everything else the other. The safest
road, the brightest road, and therefore the road where everyone is
seen.

## Canon summary

The Ember Road runs wide and warm, sharing heat with the vent-channels
beneath its floor — the one road where travelers loosen their wraps.
Its traffic is constant and its verges have grown a linear market:
licensed stalls in the wall-niches, tea-braziers at the rest-bays,
notice-boards at every hundred-span post. Wardens patrol it in pairs
and are mostly bored.

## Visible traits

Underfloor warmth shimmering the lamp-light. Niche-stalls with awnings
indoors — habit outrunning need. Notice-boards layered a finger thick
with old paper. The bell-posts' fresh rope, renewed monthly with small
ceremony.

## Systems impact

The island's food security travels here; a blockage at any point is
felt in Highroof bread queues within a day. Stall licenses are parish
patronage (factions/economy passes). News moves this road faster than
bell-code — a fact the crown's clerks resent.

## Relationships

Runs [[highroof]] ↔ [[embershare]]. Its rest-bays are the island's
informal parliament.

## Quest hooks

- A stall license was revoked overnight and reissued to a stranger —
  the ousted family wants to know whose name is on the patronage line.
- Someone is posting notices in a cipher; the wardens tear them down,
  and by next bell they are back.

## Environmental storytelling

A rest-bay bench with three generations of the same family's initials.
Warm floor-stones polished dark by bare feet in a world of boots — the
poor walk this road unshod on purpose.

## Open questions

- Which parish holds the stall-patronage, and what does it buy with it?
