## Function

Joinable guild three: the risk-and-commerce verb set (run, salvage,
cover, share, dare) and the working face of depth-vs-sky. Joining them
is learning the open — cover-craft, budget-craft, and the ethics of
what you carry home and what you leave lying.

## Canon summary

The Crews hold the salvage licenses of [[lastdoor]]: chartered teams
who run the [[open_fields]] under warden budgets, bringing down
grain-seed, glass, tools, and the Daylight's stranded wealth. Their
public creed is the Lastlight blessing *spent bright* — risk spent
well is honored, waste of it is the sin. Their private truth runs on
two ledgers: the best crews keep unlicensed lines and quiet caches
beyond the covers, and among themselves they hold an inventory of
things found out there that no manifest has ever listed — held back
not for profit, but because reporting them would invite questions the
crews cannot answer under budget.

## Visible traits

Sky-scarves and chalked crew-marks; cover-maps inked on wrap linings;
share-arithmetic argued in gate shadows. Lastlight-skewed and proud of
it, with Underfirst quartermasters keeping the licensed half of the
books — both cultures' jokes about this are old and worn smooth.

## Systems impact

The salvage economy is theirs end to end: licensing, pricing (by
exposure spent), warehouse law, and the crew-shares system that half
the island's contracts imitate. Their standing grievances — the
[[chitin_bridge]]'s double toll and the wardens' budget ceilings —
are the island's most reliable tavern politics. Joinable (spec 010,
ruled): membership in one guild bars none of the others — every guild
wants first claim on the untaken outsider, none has veto.

## Relationships

Licensed at [[lastdoor]]; budgeted (and resented) by the [[wardens]];
tolled (and resented) by the bridge-keepers of [[chitin_bridge]]; the
Quiet Lines overlap the gallery folk of [[the_scar]]. To the
[[the_untaken_arrival]] they bring the simplest want on the island: an
untaken partner rewrites every budget — the Licensed want the player
on runs, and the Quiet Lines want them off the books entirely.

## Quest hooks

- The sealed granary (see [[open_fields]]): the crew that found the
  seed gone from inside has sat on the find for a season — the player
  is offered the secret as a share.
- The roofed cache: the crew rumor at [[lastdoor]] is true, and the
  cache's roof is older than the crews — someone built cover out
  there after the Coming.
- The stranded crew: alive under cover one roof short of home (see
  [[open_fields]]) — the rescue is a budget problem, a courage
  problem, and a politics problem in that order, and the player's
  presence changes the arithmetic. (One event, two guild doors — the
  wardens hold the budget side; quests pass.)
- The quiet row, from the crews' side: the wash took a find nobody had
  offered it ([[the_shares]]) — tracing the parish key is a favor the
  Lines cannot ask for out loud.
- The bridge count: the manifest and the toll-book disagree by three
  carts a week and the discrepancy starts at the bridge — the crews
  pay the fourth taking loudest, and a documented count is a crown
  hearing waiting to be forced ([[chitin_bridge]]).

## Environmental storytelling

The crew shrine of borrowed grief-tablets for the lost-not-Taken.
Warehouse shelves where salvage sits in quarantine rows awaiting
tithe-assessment — and one row that is always empty by morning.
Cover-maps' chalk conventions taught to children as a game.
