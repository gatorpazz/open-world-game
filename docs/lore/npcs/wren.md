## Function

Town texture (spec 010): the gate's awning-mender and Lastdoor's most
reliable teller of the arrival legend — the player's mirror for what
the island says about them. Half of the gossip pair across the cut
with [[munnok]].

## Canon summary

Wren is Lastlight, hoard-named for a vanished bird, and mends the
awning-stubs that let Lastdoor roof its last open stretches in
minutes. Her stall-side seat by the Last Gate hears everything, and
she retells the [[the_untaken_arrival]] in all three versions the
season has produced — warm, dry, and exact about which teller
believes which. (Ratified, spec 010 dialogue pass — the three
tellings: the wardens' way, exposure beyond doctrine, all numbers;
the parish way, a mercy sent roofless; the crews' way, proof the sky
can be beaten. Folk attributions, not faction positions.) Asked what the nine-strokes-and-one-crossed chalk
mark means, she goes bright and vague in the same breath: a folk mark
nobody will explain to the stranger it refers to (canon), and Wren
least of all, fondly.

## Visible traits

Needle-case at the wrist where wealth would sit; awning-canvas
calluses; a sky-scarf patched from nine kinds of cloth. She works in
the gate's lee and narrates the street like weather.

## Systems impact

None beyond talk — and talk is the function: greeting texture, the
three versions, and the courtship's street-level echo. Her mending
keeps the town's minutes-to-roof promise real.

## Relationships

[[lastlight]] by descent and voice; the gate's awning-mender at
[[lastdoor]]. Trades versions across the cut with [[munnok]] —
their disagreement is a duet. Sells patchwork to [[tansy]]'s row and
gossip to everyone.

## Quest hooks

- The three versions (canon, [[the_untaken_arrival]]): hear all three
  from their believers — Wren can say who tells which, and why, for a
  favor's worth of listening.
- The sawed counterweight rope (canon, [[lastdoor]]): her lee-side
  seat faces the gate. She has seen who climbs. She mends awnings, not
  quarrels.
- The daylight canvas: the patch of true surface cloth in her kit is
  priced beyond any roof's honesty. Where a gate-lee awning-mender got
  archival-grade daylight cloth is a question worth a quiet trade.

## Environmental storytelling

Awning hooks worn bright on every lintel — her rounds, written in
brass. A patch of true daylight canvas in her kit, priced beyond any
roof's honesty. Under her seat, in chalk: nine strokes, the tenth
crossed, rubbed and redrawn.

## Open questions

- Wren's fourth version — what she privately believes happened. (Keep
  unwritten; her withholding is the character.)
- Who redraws the chalk under her seat. (Folk-mark canon; reserved
  with the mark's meaning.)
