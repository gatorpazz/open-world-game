## Function

The anchor event of all history: the arrival of the hives and the
beginning of [[the_taking]]. Everything the player walks through —
roofs, rites, laws, prices — is this event still happening slowly.

## Canon summary

The hives came in the year after [[the_still_year]], and the tellings
never agree on the first hour: some say shapes in the high blue for
days beforehand, some say a clear sky and then not. What follows is
agreed: the first Takings from the open fields, the panic of half-roofs and
cellar-nights, and the emptying of the surface in a single season as
the island learned the one law that has never since been broken — go
under, and live. The Daylight ended not in fire but in an evacuation.

## Visible traits

Today: the roofed world entire. The [[open_fields]] preserved
mid-gesture — doorways bricked half-up in that last panicked season.
Tellings that disagree about warning, and families that still define
themselves by whether their line went under early or late.

## Systems impact

The founding premise of every institution: warden doctrine, the
grief-rite, roof-law, exposure pricing. Grandmother-dating counts from
it; the Bell Rolls count from it in years ("Roll 1" is its first
winter). Arguments about the warning are arguments about blame, and
they are not academic to the families involved.

## Relationships

Follows [[the_still_year]]; begins [[the_taking]]'s reign; empties the
[[terrace_centuries]]' world; drives [[the_going_under]]. The sky's
reasons are reserved (spoiler: high).

## Quest hooks

- Two old families keep rival tellings of the first hour — early-under
  and late-under — and a marriage between them wants a single version
  for the rite. Someone has to choose what the couple's children will
  recite.

## Environmental storytelling

Half-bricked doorways. Cellar-shrines from the panic seasons, older
than the grief-rite's forms. The first winter's tally-marks on the
[[first_roof]]'s threshold — counting people in, scratched over,
counting again.

- How long did the surface hold out — the resisters, the refusers, the
