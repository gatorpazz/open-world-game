## Function

The island's great obstacle: a canyon that severs the [[last_road]] and
concentrates everything the roofed world cannot digest — smugglers,
the unlicensed, the grieving, and the galleries of the
[[drowned_stair]]. The Scar is where depth vs. sky stops being a theme
and becomes a route choice.

## Canon summary

The Scar opened in a single night, taking the Last Road's middle span
and a caravan with it, and it has never been roofed: too wide for
timber, too unstable for stone, and — the wardens' quiet addition — too
watched from above to be worth the workers. Its walls are honeycombed
with older galleries the collapse exposed, now a vertical warren of
squats, caches, and paths that change hands by knife and rumor. Its
floor holds standing water, older masonry, and the [[drowned_stair]].

## Visible traits

A slot of open sky overhead — most Vaultfolk's only sight of weather.
Daylight moving down the walls like a slow bell. Rope descents and
gallery mouths black in the sun-glare. Far below: water sheened with
lava-light from cracks, and stonework older in style than anything in
the Highroof.

## Systems impact

Crossing options price each other: the [[chitin_bridge]] (tolled, fast,
lawful) against the Scar descent (free, slow, roofless in stretches,
and subject to whoever holds the galleries this season). Exposure rules
apply on the floor — sky-priced travel inside the island's own body.
The galleries are the island's black market ([[the_shares]]).

## Relationships

Severs the [[last_road]]; crossed by the [[chitin_bridge]]; holds the
[[drowned_stair]]. Denning ground of the [[scar_hound]] packs. The
exposed older masonry predates the Coming and belongs to no era —
reserved with the [[deep_roads]].

## Quest hooks

- A gallery squat's children are guiding travelers across for bread —
  accurately, safely, and straight past a cache someone pays them to
  route around.
- The wardens want a survey of the floor's older masonry; the gallery
  folk have opinions about surveys.
- After a rockfall, a new gallery mouth opened — and the first crew
  through hasn't come back up.

## Environmental storytelling

Rope ladders in every state from new to prayer. Squat hearths in
gallery mouths, smoke-stained decades deep. The old masonry's mason
marks: confident, unfamiliar, unrepeated anywhere above. A caravan
wheel-hub in the floor-water, mortared with river stones by someone who
took the time.
