## Function

Fixes how the world reckons time with no sky to read — the frame every
schedule, counter-hour, and rite hangs on, and the canon ground under the
game clock (bells within a day, circuit-days across it) rather than a
solar day the roof does not have.

## Canon summary

The under-sun — the lava veins beneath the roof — "never sets and never
rises" [[undersun_galleries]], so there is no dawn, no dusk, no solar day
to reckon by. The island tells time instead by **bells**: the bell-code
cast and tuned at the [[bellworks]] rings across every road and gate, and
counters keep bell-hours — [[suddel]] works his on gate-bell hours, and
between bells the counter is shuttered. The longer count is the **nine-day
circuit** of the grief-rite: the [[reciters]] read every name at the
[[grief_court]] over nine days, a cycle that has never once lapsed and that
structures civic time for the whole island. A day is a round of bells; a
circuit is nine days; the sealed high-sky weeks sit above both and stay
the wardens' to declare.

## Visible traits

Bells on their ropes at every gate and road-mouth; counters shuttered
between bells; the reciters' nine-stopped staff counting the circuit
[[reciters]].

## Systems impact

The game clock reads in bells within a day and circuit-days across the
nine-day cycle — no sun meter, no light-based day/night. Counter- and
shop-hours (NPC presence) follow the bells. The number of bells in a day
is a play-balance figure, not a canon count.

## Relationships

Bells cast and named at the [[bellworks]]; the circuit kept by the
[[reciters]] at the [[grief_court]]; the sunless frame from
[[undersun_galleries]] and the roof of [[the_taking]].

## Quest hooks

- A road bell rung off its schedule (see [[bellworks]]) throws a whole
  ward's counter-hours out of true.

## Environmental storytelling

Shuttered counters and the out-crew's chalk on the shutter edge
[[suddel]]; a gate that rings its bell late.

## Open questions

  not fixed one.
