## Core promise

**The fantasy:** you are the outsider a hidden world learns to trust —
washed ashore into the roofed island of [[undervault]], climbing its
parishes and guilds until the factions ask you the only question that
matters: does the island keep hiding from the sky, or fight for it?

**The twist that is law:** [[the_taking]] — stand under open sky and the
hives will come for you; levitation is the predator's own art, stolen.
Everything bends around the roof.

**Emotional palette:** oppressive · sacred · luminous · patient · resolute.
Lamplight and liturgy pressed under an enormous silence; the roofed world
is one continuous temple.

**Forbidden clichés:**

- **No mindless swarm.** The ants are never just bugs to squash; the
  Taking has a logic — alien, perhaps never fully revealed, but present.
- **No misery-porn underground.** The roofed world is lived-in, luminous,
  occasionally funny. A district that is only grim is wrong.
- **No generic-medieval default.** Materials, dress, law, and idiom derive
  from chitin, fungus, stone, rope, and lava-glass. If it could sit
  unchanged in a stock castle game, redo it.

## Rules of the world

- **Cosmology:** the sky is not heaven — it is held territory. Warmth and
  light rise from below, from the lava veins that serve as the caverns'
  under-sun (infrastructure and mystery both; divinity status open).
- **The law:** [[the_taking]] — the rule of record for the roofed world.
- **Magic:** real, aspected, and administered by religious orders of the
  pantheon; levitation exists and is the most precious and contested art
  (shape fixed in the M2 magic pass, feeding spec 008).
- **Technology:** rope, stone, fungus-craft, chitin-wrighting, lava-glass;
  heat and light are utilities with owners.
- **Death, travel, communication:** the honored dead go down into stone —
  only the Taken go up. Travel is roofed galleries, covered under-roads,
  and priced open sprints; no open water, no unshafted flight. News moves
  by bell-code, shaft-echo, and runners.

## Historical spine

One fact is fixed: generations ago the hives came, and the island went
under roof — the Coming (name provisional). Nothing else about the past is
canon yet. The M2 timeline pass fixes the eras (this section is
`VALID_ERAS`' source of truth) and hangs 12–20 events on them, each with
present-day consequences.

## Regional map index

- **Active:** [[undervault]] — the roofed island, the whole playable world
  of the vertical slice.
- **Locked (future regions, entries to come with their passes):** the
  Hives above; the Deep Roads below; the leviathan sea around. Vertical
  regions — the island stacks rather than sprawls.
- **Travel methods:** roofed arteries and under-roads (safe, slow, tolled)
  · open-ground crossings (fast, sky-priced) · shaft-levitation inside
  enclosed verticals · no open water, no open flight.

## Peoples & powers

The people call themselves Vaultfolk (working demonym, cultures pass may
rename). The pantheon is real and its orders are tied to aspects of magic;
parishes tend the lava vents; guilds run the roofed roads, the salvage
trade, and the fisheries; a deliberately weak central figure sits at the
citadel-mountain. Cultures, factions, and the wildlife of the dark are
drafted in their M2 passes — this section grows links as they land.

## Content grammar

- **Dialogue tone defaults:** measured · euphemistic · exact · warm-dry ·
  unhurried. People under-name the danger: the hives are "the Above," the
  Taken are "kept," the surface is "the open." Saying the plain words
  reads as transgressive or intimate.
- **Naming meta-rules:** all proper nouns are provisional until the
  cultures pass writes phoneme and place-name rules; prefer descriptive,
  translatable names (the Undervault, the Coming) that a future language
  can re-coin. No real-language copies; no apostrophe gibberish.
- **Material logic:** if an object, garment, or building can't say *rope,
  stone, fungus, chitin, or lava-glass*, it must justify itself.
- **Forbidden tropes:** the Core promise bans, applied at review; hooks
  and rumors must serve at least one core tension (depth vs. sky · shelter
  vs. stagnation · grief vs. hope).
- **Lore-text markup:** cross-reference with flat `[[id]]` wikilinks only;
  quote example dialogue inside code fences so it never links.

## Player-facing surfaces

- **The `surface` contract:** every reviewed entry declares where it
  reaches the player — dialogue · journal · environment · rumor · quest —
  or `none` (legal, but audited: canon that never surfaces is rot).
- **Spoiler policy:** `high` is reserved for the sky's logic — what the
  Taking is *for*, what the hives hold, what came back down. High-spoiler
  facts stay out of ambient dialogue and out of canon packets unless the
  director overrides. `low` covers politics and secrets; `none` is common
  knowledge under the roof.
