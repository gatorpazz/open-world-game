## Function

The choke point of the island's economy and the [[last_road]]'s stitch
across [[the_scar]]: whoever holds the bridge taxes Lastdoor's whole
trade. A standing lesson that the roofed world's law has owners.

## Canon summary

The bridge is trussed from the carapaces of felled scouts — oiled
amber-black chitin lashed over a rope-and-timber spine, roofed in
overlapping plates like the shell of something vast crossing the gap
forever. Its keepers collect the toll, keep the lamps, and renew the
oil; the toll is justified as upkeep, and the chitin has never once
needed repair. Crossing it is the only way over the Scar that does not
cost sky.

## Visible traits

Amber-black gloss overhead and underfoot. Gate-shrines at both ends,
tallies scratched into the toll-posts. Wind pressing up through the
deck seams — the Scar breathing. Lamplight moving inside the chitin's
translucence like slow blood.

## Systems impact

Toll on every crossing — the fourth taking, whose answer no crown has
ever collected ([[the_shares]]); closure reroutes all traffic down
through the Scar's galleries. The keepers' allegiance —
crown, parish, or guild — is the factions pass's single most loaded
question.

## Relationships

Spans [[the_scar]] on the [[last_road]]. Built from the same salvage
that plates [[lastdoor]]'s gate and arches [[tidegate]]'s cranes.

## Quest hooks

- The toll-book and the road manifest disagree weekly (see
  [[last_road]]) — the discrepancy starts at the bridge.
- A keeper's lamp went out mid-span three nights running; the keeper
  pays for oil that never arrives, and won't say who collects it.
- Petitioners want the toll suspended for grief-processions; the
  keepers' answer will tell the island who they really serve.

## Environmental storytelling

Toll tallies deep enough to read by touch. Shrine niches at both gates
— one kept scrupulously, one not. A single carapace plate near the
midpoint that is newer than the rest, and no one collects a story about
it.
