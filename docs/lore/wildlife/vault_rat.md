## Function

The bottom of the roofed food chain and the top of every maintenance
budget: vault rats are the encounter a child can win, the pelt a poor
crew can always sell, and the reason rope — the island's structural
material — is inspected on a calendar.

## Canon summary

Vault rats live wherever the roof holds warmth and crumbs: warehouse
rows, terrace edges, gallery seams, the spaces under floor-grates.
They eat the fungus crops, the stores' spillage, and — the expensive
part — the fibers of working rope, which on a rope-hung island makes
them infrastructure's smallest enemy. Settlements post standing
bounties; quartermasters buy pelts at counted rates; a first rat is
half the island's first hunt story.

## Visible traits

Grey-brown, velvet-dusted coats; quick low runs along wall-seams.
Spoor: gnawed rope fiber in neat piles, droppings under grate-light.
Sound: the skitter under the floor that every Vaultfolk child can
tell from drip-echo by their third winter.

## Systems impact

Low-danger encounters in stores, terraces, and galleries; pelts trade
at posted bounty ([[suddel]]'s counter at Lastdoor). Rope-gnaw drives
a real maintenance economy — splicers' work, inspection calendars,
and the doubled knots that are half custom, half rat-insurance.

## Relationships

Everywhere in [[undervault]]; densest at [[lastdoor]]'s warehouse row
and [[embershare]]'s terrace rims; prey of the [[scar_hound]] packs;
bounty-priced across counters like [[suddel]]'s.

## Quest hooks

- A warehouse row's rats vanished — all of them, in a week. The
  splicers are delighted; the quartermasters, who know what empties a
  larder of rats, are not.

## Environmental storytelling

Bounty shelves of tagged pelts. Rope-ends whipped in bright thread —
inspected this season. A child's first-hunt pelt nailed proudly over
a doorway, tail and all.

## Open questions

- How deep do they range — do the galleries below the Undersun keep
  rats, and does anything down there keep them? (Deep Roads
  adjacency; reserve.)
