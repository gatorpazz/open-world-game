## Function

Town texture (spec 010): a warehouse-row hand and the Underfirst half
of the gossip pair with [[wren]] — the counted, skeptical telling of
the arrival legend, and the row's voice besides [[suddel]]'s counter.

## Canon summary

Munnok is Underfirst, roll-named, and has shifted surface goods at
[[lastdoor]]'s warehouse row long enough to price a jar of field seed
by the sound it makes set down. He tells the [[the_untaken_arrival]]
in the version with numbers in it — how long the tallies say, how many
initialed the log, how much a crossing cost that morning — and calls
the other two versions singing. His skepticism is Underfirst courtesy:
counted and kept, and nothing added.

## Visible traits

Sack-dust in the wrap seams; a counting-string worn business-plain; a
back that lifts with the knees. He stacks while he talks and never
loses either count.

## Systems impact

None beyond talk and texture: greeting coverage, the counted version
of the legend, warehouse-row color. His stacking order quietly encodes
what the assessors will see first.

## Relationships

[[underfirst]] by descent and habit; a hand of the warehouse row at
[[lastdoor]], within earshot of [[suddel]]'s counter and under
[[wester]]'s shipments. Trades tellings across the cut with [[wren]]
— he brings the numbers, she brings the weather.
