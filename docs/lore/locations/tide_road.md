## Function

The covered spoke between [[highroof]] and [[tidegate]]: the fish-run,
the salt-run, and the traveler's first lesson that roofed does not mean
safe — it means watched.

## Canon summary

The Tide Road descends from the Highroof's western gate-hall in long
rope-railed switchbacks toward the sea-caves. It is the coldest and
wettest of the three roads, weeping brine through its seams, lit by
fish-oil lamps the porters refill as a toll-in-kind. Cart traffic runs
on bell-schedule; between bells, the road belongs to whoever is quiet
on it.

## Visible traits

Green-gold lamp niches. Brine stains fanning down limewashed walls.
Rope rails slick to the touch. Drip-echo that carries a conversation
fifty spans farther than the speakers think.

## Systems impact

The catch moves on it twice daily; miss the bell-window and the fish
sour and the prices climb. Its porters' guild holds the lamp-toll
(economy pass). The dampness makes it the cheapest road to travel and
the most expensive to maintain.

## Relationships

Runs [[highroof]] ↔ [[tidegate]]. Sees smuggled rib-scrimshaw more
often than its toll-books admit.

## Quest hooks

- Between bells, someone is moving cargo that never appears at either
  end of the road.
- A lamp-porter has stopped refilling one specific niche, and travelers
  who rest in its dark are losing things.

## Environmental storytelling

A shrine-alcove where porters touch the wall in passing — the limewash
worn through to stone in one hand-shaped patch. Cart-groove detours
around a repaired collapse nobody remembers happening.
