## Function

The single source of the island's bell-code infrastructure: casting
hall, tuning gallery, and rite-house in one, hung off [[embershare]]'s
great vent. Communication, liturgy, and industry share one roof here —
the sacred-claustrophobia palette as a workplace.

## Canon summary

Every bell in the Undervault — road bells, gate bells, the wrapped
bells of the processional tunnels — is cast at the Bellworks, tuned in
its long gallery, and named before it ships. Bell-founding is a
liturgical craft: the molds are blessed, the pours timed to the vent's
rhythm, and a bell's first strike is witnessed and recorded in the
Bell Rolls, the island's most complete civic ledger. Bell-bronze is the
island's one metal that matters: a finite pre-Coming stock, recast bell
to bell, never mined, never grown — every bell is made of older bells.
Bronze that leaves the Rolls — a bell soured past recasting, scrap that
never held a voice — goes unrolled to the blade stream. A bell that sours in service is recast; a bell that rings
wrong from birth is a scandal, and there has just been one.

## Visible traits

The casting hall's vent-glare and shadow-play. Bell-blanks on racks,
stamped with casting years. The tuning gallery: a hundred spans of
hung bronze, struck softly, endlessly, by apprentices with felted
mallets. The Rolls — shelf on shelf of strike-records — behind a
lava-glass wall.

## Systems impact

Bell-code is the island's nervous system; the Bellworks holds its
integrity. The Rolls double as a census of public works (the Bell
Rolls date the eras' public record). Founding rights are parish-held
([[vent_parishes]]); the founders dispute the margins with no charter
of their own.

## Relationships

Within [[embershare]], on the great vent; its bells hang on every
road and gate; its Rolls cross-reference the [[grief_court]]'s
recitation calendar.

## Quest hooks

- The wrong-ringing bell: cast true, blessed true, and it strikes a
  tone the tuning gallery cannot name — the founders want it recast,
  the reciters want it studied, and the Rolls page for its pour is
  missing.
- An apprentice's felt mallet came back from the gallery smelling of
  brine — nobody tunes bells for [[tidegate]] this season.
- A road bell's rope was found re-hung a hand higher — someone shorter
  than the bell-warden is ringing schedules.

## Environmental storytelling

Blessing-marks chalked on molds, washed off after every true pour —
one mold's mark scrubbed so hard the stone is dished. The gallery's
felt-soft thunder. A shelf-gap in the Rolls, dusted around, not in.
