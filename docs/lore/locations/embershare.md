## Function

The island's breadbasket and forge-town: fungal terrace farms descending
its great shaft, smithies rings around the vent, and the [[bellworks]]
casting every bell-code bell in the Undervault. Embershare is where
parish politics are thickest — whoever tends the vent feeds the island.

## Canon summary

Embershare is a warren cut around the island's strongest lava vent, its
terraces stepping down toward the glow like a lit amphitheater. Heat is
held as a commons — the "share" of its name — measured out by the vent's
parish in baking hours, forge hours, and warmth-rights. Its terraces
grow the fungus staples; its ovens are altars; its smiths temper
lava-glass tools no other town can match. One high tier stands cold and
abandoned, and the parish does not discuss why.

## Visible traits

Terrace rims glowing bronze from below. Oven-mouths in rows, each with
its blessing-mark. Fungal velvet in cultivated colors — dye is an
Embershare luxury. Bell-blanks cooling on racks. The abandoned tier
above: swept clean, doors ajar, warm air rising past it untouched.

## Systems impact

Staple food and all worked lava-glass originate here; the [[ember_road]]
to the Highroof is the island's busiest artery. Embershare's commons and the Highroof's deeded light
([[undersun_galleries]]) are rival legal theories — the island has
never chosen between them, which keeps its lawyers fed (economy pass).
If the vent guttered, the Undervault would starve in a season — a fact
everyone knows and no one says aloud.

## Relationships

Feeds and arms [[highroof]] via the [[ember_road]]. Home of the
[[bellworks]]. Its vent-parish and smith-guild take shape in the
factions pass.

## Quest hooks

- The vent's glow has shifted color one hour in twenty — the parish
  calls it seasonal; a terrace-farmer's yield ledger says otherwise.
- The abandoned high tier: a family petitions to resettle it, and the
  parish keeps losing their paperwork.
- A smith is selling "Embershare-tempered" lava-glass that never saw the
  vent — and the real smiths want it handled quietly, not lawfully.

## Environmental storytelling

Blessing-marks refreshed on every oven but one. A terrace rail polished
by two hundred years of the same resting hands. The cold tier's swept
floors — someone maintains what no one inhabits. Bell-blanks stamped
with the years of their casting: none from the years of the Coming.
