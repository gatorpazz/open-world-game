## Function

The covered spoke between [[highroof]] and [[lastdoor]] — severed
mid-length by [[the_scar]] and stitched back together by the
[[chitin_bridge]]. The road that makes the island's politics physical:
every salvaged jar pays the bridge before it pays the crown.

## Canon summary

The Last Road was built straight and generous in the years the surface
trade mattered most — then the ground opened under its middle span and
took a caravan with it, and the road has ended twice ever since. Its
two halves are sound, patrolled, and busy; the crossing between them
belongs to the bridge and the bridge's keepers. Old road-markers still
count the distance as if the gap were not there.

## Visible traits

Generous width gone shabby: dead lamp niches, patched floor, tidy
masonry giving way to expedient timber and rope near the break. Wind —
the only roofed road where a traveler feels moving air, breathing up
from the Scar through the seams.

## Systems impact

All Lastdoor salvage and all warden logistics move here and pay the
bridge toll — a double taxation Lastdoor resents loudly
([[the_shares]]). When the bridge closes, the only alternative is down through the
Scar's galleries: slower, roofless in stretches, and lawless.

## Relationships

Runs [[highroof]] ↔ [[lastdoor]], broken at [[the_scar]], crossing at
[[chitin_bridge]].

## Quest hooks

- The road-wardens' manifest and the bridge-keepers' toll-book disagree
  by three carts a week, every week, in the same direction.
- A road-marker near the break has been re-carved to count a distance
  that no longer exists — freshly.

## Environmental storytelling

The caravan shrine at the break's lip: wheel-hubs set in mortar, one
per lost cart. Timber shoring inscribed with its donors' names —
generosity as insurance. Wind-worn rope where the roof meets the gap.
