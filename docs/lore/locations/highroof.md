## Function

The island's one city and the game's central hub: seat of the weak crown,
home of the great grief-court, terminus of all three spoke roads. Social
climbing happens here — the parishes and guilds keep their high seats
under the Highroof, and the question the factions will one day ask the
player gets asked in its halls.

## Canon summary

The Highroof is a mountain worked into a building over generations:
buttressed faces, shuttered galleries, and no open windows above the
third reach. Its wards stack — forge-floors and markets low near the
[[undersun_galleries]], residential terraces in the middle heights, and
above them the [[sealed_reaches]], mortared shut from the inside. The
city grew upward from the [[first_roof]], the refuge cavern of the
Coming, which survives beneath its lowest ward as a shrine. The crown
sits here because everyone agrees it should sit somewhere harmless.

## Visible traits

From outside: a peak that is visibly architecture, limewashed and blind
above the third reach. Inside: lamplit galleries with upward-cast
shadows, rope banisters worn to silk, chitin-trussed market vaults,
processional tunnels strung with wrapped bells. Ward boundaries marked in
lava-glass mosaic underfoot.

## Systems impact

All three roads ([[tide_road]], [[ember_road]], [[last_road]]) meet at
its gate-halls — most travel passes through the Highroof whether it
wants to or not. Services concentrate here: high shrines, guild seats,
the only licensed levitation shafts. Ward tolls fund the roof-wardens.
Rents rise with distance from the light-wells.

## Relationships

Contains [[grief_court]], [[sealed_reaches]], [[undersun_galleries]],
and [[first_roof]] beneath. Fed by [[embershare]]'s terraces and
[[tidegate]]'s catch. The crown's shape and the guilds' seats are the
factions pass's work.

## Quest hooks

- A ward toll-keeper is waving through unmarked crates from the
  [[last_road]] at night — bribed, threatened, or devout?
- The crown quietly pays for reports of sounds inside the
  [[sealed_reaches]]; a new report has gone missing along with its
  reporter.
- A light-rights dispute: a middle ward's mirrors were re-angled toward a
  high shrine, and a terrace of homes has gone dim.

## Environmental storytelling

Mortared window-scars climbing the upper faces. A gate-hall floor grooved
by generations of cart wheels, with newer grooves detouring around a
walled-off arch. Wrapped bells everywhere — and one bell, high in the
crown's tower, that is not wrapped and never rings.
