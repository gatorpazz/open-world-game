## Function

The edge-informer: a weathered catcher who works the covered edges and
quiet galleries around [[lastdoor]], selling caged hush-moths to the
warden post. He is the town's quiet authority on what moves along the
covers — and a first lesson in Lastlight wariness: his greeting is
warm and his grounds are nobody's business.

## Canon summary

Swift is Lastlight, named from the word-hoard for a vanished bird that
took insects on the wing — half advertisement, half elegy, and
everyone can hear both. He hangs cages where regard runs and moths
follow, harvests them by lamp-dark, and sells to the [[wardens]] by
the cage; a good catching-ground is guarded knowledge, and his are the
best in the gate-country. He has walked the covers longer than most
crews and outlasted two that wanted him on the books.

## Visible traits

Fungal-bright wraps gone grey at the seams; the sky-scarf worn loose;
cage-poles slung like fishing rods. He moves quietly out of habit, not
craft, and shakes wing-dust from lamp-hoods in one practiced motion.
Found at his stall-door by the warden post at bell-hours, and nowhere
in particular otherwise.

## Systems impact

Supplies the warden post's moth-cages — the detection infrastructure
of [[the_workings]]' law — and prices his catch by the cage. Knows
the covered approaches' rumor before the notice-boards do. Will not
guide, but will say where not to walk, which is worth more.

## Relationships

[[lastlight]] by descent and song; catcher of [[hush_moths]]; sole
regular supplier to [[lastdoor]]'s warden post ([[wardens]]); works
grounds toward [[the_scar]]'s quieter mouths and tells no one which.

## Quest hooks

- His best ground has gone silent — no moths at all, for days. He
  wants a witness to walk it with him and say nothing to the wardens
  until he understands what a silence like that is.
- The warden post doubled its standing order and will not say why it
  suddenly needs so many cages. Swift cannot fill it alone and does
  not trust help he didn't choose.
- Someone has been following him toward his grounds. He wants them led
  wrong — convincingly, kindly, and without a fight.

## Environmental storytelling

Cage-hooks in a row by his door, two always empty. Wing-dust films on
his lamp-hoods, wiped in the lighting motion. Outside his stall, a
child's chalk moth-fall game, copied badly, which he has never once
scrubbed off.

## Open questions

- Which family telling promised him the bird-name Swift, and who bore
  it before him. (Hoard custom; director taste.)
- Where his grounds actually are. (Quest-level; the answer is a map
  someone would pay for.)
