## Function

The timeline's last entry and the game's inciting fact: the player's
arrival as the island records it. Written from the island's point of
view — what the Undervault knows, disputes, and wants; not what is
true. The main-quest spine hangs from this event.

## Canon summary

In the current Roll-year, wardens at [[lastdoor]] found a stranger
lying in the center of the [[bait_square]] — exposed under full sky,
by every reading of the tallies, for longer than doctrine records any
living thing enduring, and untaken. No crossing of the sea is
recorded, claimed, or explained; no roof between the water and the
square sheltered them. The wardens logged what they could, disagreed
about the rest, and have not amended the tallies. The stranger woke.

## Visible traits

Today: the newest legend, still soft at the edges — told in three
versions within a season of happening. The new scorch-ring drawn
around where the stranger lay. The tally-log's two initialed versions.
Every parish and guild finding polite reasons to meet the stranger.

## Systems impact

Doctrine's authority rests on its tables covering everything; this
event is the uncovered case, and the wardens' response — retest,
revere, or revise — is a live institutional fight. The factions'
courtship of the player begins here: an outsider owed by no one,
readable by everyone.

## Relationships

Recorded at the [[bait_square]] by [[lastdoor]]'s wardens; strains
[[warden_doctrine]]; the event [[undervault]]'s Function turns on.
What actually happened — sea, fields, and sky — is the game's reserved
mystery (spoiler: high; spec 010 owns the opening's staging).

## Quest hooks

- The warden-captain's request stands: return to the square under
  witness, for the tallies. The town has taken sides about whether
  that is science, trial, or bait.

## Environmental storytelling

The reverence-or-quarantine ring. Chalk starting to appear on walls
near the Last Gate: nine strokes, a tenth crossed through — a folk
mark nobody will explain to the stranger it refers to.
